How To Play Memory Game

The goal is to match all of the tiles to their pair.

For each turn, two tiles will be selected. If they match, they will remain flipped. Otherwise, they will be turned back over.

When all of the tiles are matched and turned over, the game is done. The lower your number of turns, the better your score!

At the end of each game, a Statistics Dialog will open. Pressing the Esc key will close the Statistics dialog and the Memory Tile game.

To change your username from the default assignment, click on the "Player" option at the top of the screen

Good luck!
